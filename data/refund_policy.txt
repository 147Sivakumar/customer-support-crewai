# TechNova Solutions - Refund and Return Policy

## Return Eligibility
- Customers can request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving the product.
- Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
- The original invoice or proof of purchase must be provided.
- Products damaged by customer misuse are not eligible for return.

## Refund Eligibility
Refunds are approved after the returned product passes inspection.

## Refund Processing Time
- Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
- The refund is credited to the original payment method.
- Bank processing times may vary.

## Damaged Products
Customers who receive damaged products should contact support within 48 hours of delivery.

They should provide:
- Order ID
- Product photographs
- Description of the damage

## Non-Returnable Products
- Digital products.
- Downloadable software.
- Products marked as non-returnable.

## Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, customers must follow the return policy.
