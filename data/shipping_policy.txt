# TechNova Solutions - Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Locations
We currently provide domestic shipping within India.

International shipping availability is not officially confirmed.

## Delivery Time
- Standard Shipping: 5 to 7 business days.
- Express Shipping: 2 to 3 business days.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on location and external circumstances.

## Shipping Charges
- Standard Shipping: Free for orders above ₹999.
- Standard Shipping: ₹50 for orders below ₹999.
- Express Shipping: ₹150 per order.

## Order Tracking
Customers receive a tracking link through email after their order has been shipped.

Customers can use this tracking link to check their delivery status.

## Delayed Orders
If an order has not arrived after the estimated delivery period:
1. Check the tracking link.
2. Verify the delivery address.
3. Contact customer support with the order ID.

## Lost Shipments
Customers should contact support if the tracking status has not changed for more than 5 business days.

Our support team will investigate the shipment.
