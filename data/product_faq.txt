# TechNova Solutions - Product Frequently Asked Questions

## Products Available
TechNova Solutions sells:
- Laptops
- Keyboards
- Computer Mice
- Headphones
- Monitors
- Mobile Accessories

## Laptop FAQ

Q: Do your laptops come with a warranty?
A: Yes. Laptops come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty.

Q: Can I return a laptop?
A: Yes. Eligible laptops can be returned within 7 calendar days according to our return policy.

## Headphones FAQ

Q: Do headphones have a warranty?
A: Yes. Headphones include a 6-month manufacturer warranty.

Q: Can I return opened headphones?
A: Returns are subject to product eligibility and the condition requirements in our return policy.

## Keyboard FAQ

Q: What is the warranty period for keyboards?
A: Keyboards include a 6-month manufacturer warranty.

## Mouse FAQ

Q: What is the warranty period for computer mice?
A: Computer mice include a 6-month manufacturer warranty.
